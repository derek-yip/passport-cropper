# Passport Photo Cropper

## Description

The Passport Photo Cropper is a user-friendly and efficient online tool designed to simplify the process of cropping passport photos. It eliminates the need for complex photo editing software or the hassle of visiting a professional photo studio. With just a few simple steps, users can upload their photos and make adjustments to meet the necessary dimensions.

## Features

- **Privacy and Data Security:** The Passport Photo Cropper prioritizes privacy, ensuring that user data and photos are not stored or saved in any database. Your personal information remains completely private and secure throughout the cropping process.

- **User-Friendly Interface:** The tool boasts a user-friendly interface that makes it simple and intuitive to navigate. Clear instructions and prompts guide users through the cropping and adjustment process, making it easy for everyone, regardless of their technical expertise.

- **Streamlined Workflow:** The Passport Photo Cropper eliminates the need for complex photo editing software or professional assistance. It provides a straightforward and efficient workflow, saving users valuable time and effort. Whether you're applying for a visa, renewing your passport, or any other official documentation, the Passport Photo Cropper ensures a smooth and hassle-free experience.

- **Responsive Design:** The Passport Photo Cropper is designed to be responsive, allowing users to access and use the tool on various devices. Whether you're using a desktop computer, tablet, or mobile phone, the tool adapts to the screen size, providing a seamless user experience. Crop your passport photos on the go without the need for a computer or specialized software.

## Getting Started

1. Visit the Passport Photo Cropper website at [https://passport-cropper.vercel.app/](https://passport-cropper.vercel.app/)
2. Click on the "Upload Photo" button to select your passport photo.
3. Make necessary adjustments, such as rotating, flipping, and resizing the photo.
4. Select the layout of photo for preview
5. Click "Download" icon to download the photo into local machine
6. Say goodbye to the complexities of traditional photo editing software and enjoy the convenience of the Passport Photo Cropper.
